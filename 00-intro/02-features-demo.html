Features demo

A short tour of the elements available throughout the site, so the rest of the notes can use them freely.

Mathematics

Inline math like $\nu_\text{obs} = \nu_\text{rest} / (1+z)$ and display math share the same notation; rendering is done client-side by KaTeX, which means the source stays plain LaTeX and survives copy-paste:

$$ \nu_\text{obs} \;=\; \frac{\nu_\text{rest}}{1 + z} $$

For the H30α recombination line, $\nu_\text{rest} = 231.9$ GHz; for a source at $z \approx 0.003$ the observed frequency falls near the middle of ALMA Band 6.

Interactive figures

Sliders driving live readouts and Plotly plots can be embedded inline inside a note. Below: drag the redshift slider and watch the observed frequency move along the $\nu_\text{obs}(z)$ curve. ALMA Band 6 spans $211$–$275$ GHz, so the line falls out of the band a little above $z \approx 0.099$.

The recipe for one of these is a few lines of declarative YAML — no JavaScript on the author side. Variables in the sliders block can be used in the readouts and plot expressions; Math.* functions (log, sin, exp, sqrt, PI, …) are available unqualified.

Two-column comparisons

Two short paragraphs side-by-side, separated by a --- line. Useful for before/after, A versus B, method versus result. Stacks to a single column on narrow screens.

Single-cube pipeline (legacy)

S/N was driven by the line cube only. Mask leakage from continuum was invisible. Numbers below $5\sigma$ tended to be optimistic.

Dual-cube pipeline (current)

Mask is transferred from a strong-tracer cube (CO); baseline is measured on line-free channels of the same SPW. A cross-cube consistency check is performed before the final S/N.
