Footprint and IoU — tried, then dropped

A short retrospective on two diagnostics that were on the page until 2026-05-23 and have since been removed from the active methodology. Kept here for historical context; not load-bearing for any number the pipeline currently reports.

CO 3σ footprint

The footprint was the set of pixels where the CO moment-$0$, smoothed to a $1''$ reference scale, exceeds $3\sigma$. The pipeline used to AND every candidate scan mask with this region so that the optimiser could not place a mask outside where CO emission was physically confirmed. It was introduced in late April 2026 to fix two specific incidents (NGC 7793: a $1250$-beam mask absorbing primary-beam edge noise; NGC 1313: a single-beam noise hotspot).

A separate rule was tightened the same week: the spatial mask must come from an independent strong tracer (CO), not from the HRL cube itself. That rule already eliminates the most violent failure mode this kind of constraint exists to prevent — self-masking, where the optimiser would otherwise pick HRL noise spikes as both signal and mask. Once self-masking is forbidden by construction and the scan picks the smoothing-and-threshold combination that maximises S/N, the optimal mask sits inside the would-be footprint anyway. The footprint AND-step turned out to change the verdict on zero galaxies in the 32-source sample. Removed on 2026-05-23.

IoU

IoU was the ratio of the scan mask area to the footprint area (equivalent, under the mask-inside-footprint constraint, to the fraction of the footprint the mask actually fills). It was reported in every per-galaxy summary as a morphology label and a selector-bias filter. In practice it changed the verdict on four sources in the marginal-S/N band (NGC 7582, NGC 5643, NGC 1377, IRAS F18293-3413), all of which were independently caught by the per-galaxy null and the fixed-envelope diagnostic. Removed on 2026-05-23 along with the footprint.

The actual lesson

Defending these two steps — and especially constructing an interactive demo to make the footprint look load-bearing — illustrated a pattern worth recording: a methodology accumulates overlapping safeguards, and each safeguard accumulates a defence, even when the underlying failure mode has been ruled out by a different rule. Going forward the project's default stance is subtraction — a new step has to name the galaxies whose verdict it would change vs. the existing pipeline, and a step that survives only as belt-and-suspenders for something already prevented should be dropped. The mask-from-CO rule, the per-galaxy null test, the fixed-envelope diagnostic, the baseline-residual diagnostic, and the canonical ellipse-CoG flux are the load-bearing pieces; the footprint AND-step and the IoU column are not.
