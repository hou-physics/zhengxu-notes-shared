About this archive

Everything under Archive is a snapshot of the project as it stood in May 2026, before the survey redirection agreed with Prof. Bertoldi on 2026-07-10.

At that time the project was framed as a detection experiment: find individual galaxies with secure recombination-line detections, using a parameter-scan + null-test framework. In July the goal changed to a uniform archival survey: every source is measured with one frozen procedure and contributes either a flux or a meaningful upper limit to the L(HRL)–L(TIR) correlation. The scan and per-galaxy null-test machinery described in these archived pages is therefore no longer the active method — it survives as part of the methodology-evolution narrative in the thesis.

The current method is documented step by step in Survey pipeline (sidebar above). These pages remain here unchanged for reference and for the history of how the method evolved.
