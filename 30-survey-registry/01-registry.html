Survey registry — all 142 targets

Survey registry — every target in one table

This is the survey's master bookkeeping table: every galaxy the survey has measured, reviewed, excluded — or could measure next. It is machine-generated from the pipeline's stage outputs (_step3/survey_registry.py in the master repository; source of truth result_v2/survey_registry.csv) and is regenerated after every batch run — never edited by hand. Snapshot below: 2026-08-26.

Where the rows come from

Measured/excluded rows are the pipeline's own outputs. Candidate rows descend from the selection chain (see pipeline Step 1): IRAS bright galaxies (60 μm flux > 5.24 Jy) × DEC ≤ +40° × Spitzer 8 μm coverage × usable ALMA archival data → minus those already processed.

How to read the columns

  • f60: IRAS 60 μm flux from the parent catalog (the selection quantity). Blank = target predates the catalog-based selection.
  • F, σ_F, S/N, W: the recombination-line measurement (flux, uncertainty, significance, integration width). σ_F and S/N are quoted on the calibrated scale of the thesis — every uncertainty carries the global factor 1.5 measured on the survey's own signal-free sources (see pipeline Step 4); the registry CSV stores the nominal values and the conversion is exact (×1.5 / ÷1.5). Negative F for upper limits is normal noise behaviour.
  • RMS10: measured noise per 10 km/s channel — the depth actually achieved. RMS10 pred: what the ALMA archive promised for candidate data; after processing, the two columns can be compared.
  • IR coverage: whether the ALMA field of view covers the galaxy's infrared emission (covered / covered-unresolved / partial).
  • next step: what a successor should do with this row — done, a review instruction, or a download-and-process instruction with the estimated data volume.

The full 23-column version (positions, aperture areas, infrared sizes, data-volume estimates, per-column key) lives in result_v2/survey_registry.{csv,md}.
